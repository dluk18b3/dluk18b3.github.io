Dramory Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Dramory is a personal whisky tracker for managing a collection, wishlist, and tasting notes.

Data We Handle

The current version of Dramory does not collect, transmit, sell, or share user data with third parties.

Information entered by users—such as whisky names, notes, ratings, flavour tags, and bottle details—is stored only locally on the user’s device. It is not sent to the developer or to external servers.

The app does not use:

  • account registration or authentication;
  • analytics or advertising SDKs;
  • advertising;
  • location data;
  • access to contacts, photos, camera, or microphone;
  • cookies or cross-site tracking technologies.

Data Deletion

Users can delete records within the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data in accordance with the device operating system’s settings and policies.

Because the current version of Dramory does not create accounts or transmit data to a server, no separate account-deletion request is required.

Changes to This Policy

If future versions introduce authentication, synchronization, backups, or any other transfer of data to a server, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is released.
